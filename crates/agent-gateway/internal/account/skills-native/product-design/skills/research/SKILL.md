# Research a product problem

Define the question, audience, and decisions the research should inform. Use approved public research only for the public topic provided by the user. Work from captured evidence, uploaded examples, and project facts in private synthesis. Rank observations by severity and frequency when supported. For each finding, record the source, the user's obstacle, and a concrete implication. Mark hypotheses and evidence gaps separately.
